## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Mithra is your intelligent daily companion that helps you take control of your time, build lasting habits, and stay focused on what matters most.

🧠 AI-Powered Smart Scheduling
Let Mithra's AI assistant help you organize your day. Get intelligent suggestions for task prioritization, optimal scheduling, and workload management based on your patterns and preferences.

📋 Task Management
Create, organize, and track tasks effortlessly. Set priorities, deadlines, and categories. Mithra learns from your productivity patterns to suggest the best times for different types of work.

📅 Smart Calendar
View your schedule at a glance with an intuitive calendar. Drag and drop tasks, set reminders, and never miss an important deadline again.

📝 Daily Journal
Reflect on your day with the built-in journal. Track your mood, write about achievements, and build a personal growth log that helps you understand your patterns.

🎯 Habit Tracking
Build and maintain positive habits with visual streak tracking. Set daily, weekly, or custom goals and watch your consistency grow over time.

🔕 Focus Mode (Dost Mode)
Enter distraction-free focus sessions with customizable timers. Chat with your AI companion during breaks for motivation and productivity tips.

🔔 Smart Notifications
Get timely reminders for tasks, habits, and scheduled activities. Mithra sends intelligent notifications that respect your focus time.

Key Features:
• AI-powered task scheduling and optimization
• Interactive calendar with drag-and-drop
• Habit tracking with streaks and analytics
• Daily journaling with mood tracking
• Focus mode with customizable timers
• Beautiful dark-themed UI
• Works offline — your data stays on your device
• No ads, no data selling
